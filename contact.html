FAQ

Common questions

Are you open to research collaborations?

Yes—I'm always interested in collaborations that combine NMR spectroscopy with computational methods, especially in chiral recognition, supramolecular systems, or method development. Please reach out with a brief description of your project.

Do you accept visiting students or interns?

This depends on availability and alignment with ongoing projects. Feel free to send a CV and a short motivation letter.

Can you share data or code from your publications?

Yes—most of my recent work includes open data or code in the supporting information. If something isn't publicly available, contact me and I'll do my best to share it.

Are you available for talks or workshops?

I'm happy to discuss invited talks, seminars, or workshops on diffusion NMR, GNAT, or multiscale simulation of chiral systems. Please use the contact form.
