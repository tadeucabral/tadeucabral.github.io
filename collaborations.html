Collaborations

Science is a team sport

My research would not be possible without the generosity of collaborators across disciplines—from synthetic chemistry and materials science to computational physics.

Collaboration philosophy

I believe the most interesting problems live at the boundaries between fields. Whether you work on chiral supramolecular systems, method development for NMR, or multiscale simulation, I am always open to conversations that begin with "what if we tried…".

If you believe there is a natural synergy with your work, please get in touch.
